01 — Our company

Building homes. Bringing people together.

Ehsan Plant & Property Sdn Bhd is a Bumiputera property developer and CIDB G7 contractor, established on 15 May 2008 under registration number 817795-X.

From experience to new possibilities.

Led by Dato' Haji Abdul Hamid Bin P.V Abdu (D.I.M.P), Ehsan develops homes and commercial spaces, alongside the infrastructure that supports them.

His journey in property began in 1992 with auctioneering. In 2008, he expanded into property development, bringing that experience into a business focused on the needs of homeowners and communities.

We work with public and private partners to deliver quality, accessible developments, guided by a simple promise: We Build for Your Needs.
