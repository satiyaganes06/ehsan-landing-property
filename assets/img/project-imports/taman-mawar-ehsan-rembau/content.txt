Taman Mawar Ehsan @ Rembau

1. Overview:
Taman Mawar Ehsan is a prestigious Malay Reserved residential development located in Rembau. Featuring modern landed homes, the project emphasizes comfort, practicality, and community living.

2. Property Details:
The development consists of 1-storey and 2-storey terrace homes across four phases. The 1-storey terrace houses offer a built-up area of 1,100 sqft with 3 bedrooms and 2 bathrooms, while the 2-storey terrace houses span 2,000 sqft, comprising 4 bedrooms and 3 bathrooms.

3. Highlights:
With its strategic location, spacious layouts, and serene environment, Taman Mawar Ehsan is an ideal choice for families seeking a harmonious lifestyle in Rembau. The project ensures exclusivity and long-term value through its Malay Reserved status.
