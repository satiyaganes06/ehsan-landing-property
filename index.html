01 — Doctrine

Building with purpose.

Company vision

To be a progressive and trusted Bumiputera property and construction company, recognised for quality, innovation, and our contribution to Malaysia’s economic growth.

A Company objective

  1. To develop modern, sustainable, and environmentally responsible properties.
  2. To deliver quality properties at affordable and competitive prices.
  3. To contribute to economic growth by creating employment and supporting local communities.

B Company mission

  1. To provide accessible and affordable property solutions that bring our belief, Everyone Can Own a House, closer to reality.
  2. To strengthen our position in Malaysia’s real estate and construction industry through continuous improvement and responsible growth.
  3. To stay true to our promise, We Build for Your Needs, by creating solutions shaped around the needs of the people we serve.
