01 — Identity

Who are we.

Ehsan Plant & Property Sdn Bhd was registered on 15 May 2008 under company registration number 817795-X. The company is led by Dato' Haji Abdul Hamid Bin P.V Abdu (D.I.M.P), who is committed to exploring opportunities to further elevate the company's reputation.

The company also maintains an open stance to collaborate with any party, whether government or private, to enhance the potential of the company's estates. Today it operates as a Bumiputera property developer and a CIDB G7 Bumiputera contractor — designing, financing and building its own schemes, and carrying out the infrastructure and groundwork beneath them.

1992 — Where it began

Dato' Haji Abdul Hamid Bin P.V Abdu (D.I.M.P) ventured into the property sector in 1992, eventually establishing the largest auction company in Malaysia, Ehsan Auctioneers Sdn Bhd. Ehsan Auctioneers successfully auctioned over 100,000 properties and has collaborated with every bank in Malaysia. In 2008 he expanded into property development by establishing Ehsan Property Sdn Bhd.

10 — Groundwork

Infrastructure, construction & ground work.

As a CIDB G7 Bumiputera contractor, Ehsan Plant & Property carries out its own earthworks and infrastructure rather than subcontracting the whole package — slope protection, drainage, sewerage plant, site roads and water storage.

That vertical integration is what has let the company take on abandoned schemes — Apartment Dimensi and Taman Pinggiran Markisa were both taken over mid-build and carried through to Certificate of Fitness.
