01 — Doctrine

Objective, mission, vision.

Company vision

Ehsan Plant & Property Sdn Bhd aims to provide services and products according to established standards and produce work results with the highest quality, while also contributing to the country's economic development — and aspires to be a progressive and advanced Bumiputera company in property construction.

A Company objective

  1. To create environmentally friendly and contemporary modern property constructions.
  2. To establish affordable properties.
  3. To generate more job opportunities.

B Company mission

  1. To provide affordable asset ownership facilities to the public — Everyone Can Own A House — together with Ehsan Property.
  2. To become a competitive leader in the real estate industry in Malaysia.
  3. To always consider the company motto We Build For Your Needs.
  4. To produce high-quality work results.
  5. To ensure customer satisfaction.
  6. To carry out work professionally and ethically.
